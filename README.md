## 📌 Overview
**ShilpSetu** is an AI-driven platform designed to bridge the gap between traditional artisans and global markets. By simplifying product cataloging, digital story-telling, and direct market access, ShilpSetu empowers local craftsmen to showcase their handiwork, manage inventories, and connect with buyers seamlessly.

## 📲 Account Setup & Registration
### Step 1: Navigate to Sign Up
* Once installed, open the app. On the login screen, locate **"Don't have an account?"** at the bottom and tap **Sign Up**.
<div align="center">
  <img src="WhatsApp Image 2026-09-24 at 2.56.57 PM.jpeg" alt="Sign Up Button" width="300"/>
</div>
### Step 2: Fill in Your Details
* Enter your **Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, and Password**.
* Select your specific **Craft / Business Category** and enter your **Pickup Address**. 
* Tap **Create Account** at the bottom.

## 🚀 Welcome to Your Dashboard
Once your account is created, you will land on your personalized dashboard! 
* **Quick Sell AI:** Start listing crafts immediately using the **"Take Photo & Sell"** feature to let AI create your listing.
* **Track Your Business:** Monitor performance with quick glances at **Alerts, Performance percentage, and Earnings**.
* **Quick Actions:** Manage inventory via **My Products** or track shipments under **My Orders**.
