## 📌 Overview

**ShilpSetu** is an AI-driven platform designed to bridge the gap between traditional artisans and global markets. By simplifying product cataloging, digital story-telling, and direct market access, ShilpSetu empowers local craftsmen to showcase their handiwork, manage inventories, and connect with buyers seamlessly.

## 📲 Account Setup & Registration

### Step 1: Navigate to Sign Up
* Once the app is installed, open it. On the login screen, locate the text **"Don't have an account?"** at the bottom and tap on **Sign Up**[cite: 3].

<div align="center">
  <img src="WhatsApp Image 2026-09-24 at 2.56.57 PM.jpeg" alt="Sign Up Button" width="300"/>
</div>

### Step 2: Fill in Your Details
* You will be taken to the registration form. Fill out the required details, including your **Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, and Password**[cite: 4].
* Make sure to select your specific **Craft / Business Category** and enter your **Pickup Address**[cite: 4]. 
* Once everything is filled, tap **Create Account** at the bottom[cite: 4].

  <img src="WhatsApp Image 2026-09-24 at 2.57.41 PM.jpeg" alt="Registration Form" width="300"/>
</div>

## 🚀 Welcome to Your Dashboard

Once your account is created, you will land on your personalized dashboard! 

* **Quick Sell AI:** You can immediately start listing your crafts using the **"Take Photo & Sell"** feature, which lets AI automatically create your listing[cite: 5].
* **Track Your Business:** Monitor your performance with quick glances at your **Alerts, Performance percentage, and Earnings**[cite: 5].
* **Quick Actions:** Easily manage your inventory via **My Products** or track live shipments under **My Orders**[cite: 5].
